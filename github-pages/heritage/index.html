About us

Small beginning.
Big flavour.

Chaat Junction was born in Abu Dhabi in 2020, during a challenging year, with one clear purpose: bring the irresistible flavours of Indian street food to the city with care for taste, presentation and quality.

The warm response led to a second location in 2022. In 2024, the journey grew again with sister brand Chatore Restaurant ↗—another expression of the food and hospitality guests had come to love.
